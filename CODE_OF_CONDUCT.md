# Code of conduct

VISOR contributors and maintainers are expected to create a respectful,
professional, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and patient.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Protect private data found in logs, screenshots, and process inventories.
- Accept moderation decisions made to protect the project and its community.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and
deliberate disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to `benz.on.contact@gmail.com`. Maintainers may
edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when necessary.
